Front 1 · Morning Feed

The feed takes your ⭐ and ✕, and names the reporter

  • A ⭐ or ✕ on a feed card is noted, and the card says so. Those answers count in the next edition. live #18738
  • A site that is not a news outlet is never credited with a story, and no story reaches a reader without its text. live #18755
  • A card names the story's reporter, and the feed suggests only people as reporters to pitch. live #18701 #18787
  • The Team page chooses who gets the Morning Feed. live #18690
  • War Room's take is offered on every story the page draws, not only the top picks. ships in the 24 November evening release #18853
  • An author page, marked Provisional, is behind a switch that is still off. built, off #18740
Front 2 · The chat

The floating chat answers from War Room's own data

  • Questions go to the server's agent, not a browser shortcut. "Brief me" answers in the chat and no longer writes a Workspace document. live #18759
  • Advice, rival and draft questions read SWOT, news and bills first. A filed bill is never called a hearing, and drafts speak for the client. live
  • Links to War Room data open in place, and the news tool leaves out the stories the feed refuses. ships in the 24 November evening release
Front 3 · Sign-in and security

A stranger can no longer lock you out

  • Wrong passwords typed by someone else no longer lock the real owner out of sign-in. live #16862
  • Sign-in limits count the visitor, never the shared address of the web front door. A refused try no longer counts towards the network cap. live
  • /admin opens on your own account, and the shared login is one click away and remembered per browser. ships in the 24 November evening release #18902
Front 4 · Reliability and cost

Fewer failures, faster admin pages, models kept current

  • SWOT synthesis stops failing on one bad character in a story. ships in the 24 November evening release #18852
  • The admin cost pages open in about 4 seconds, down from 16 seconds or a 25-second timeout. live #18553
  • Pictures move to a newer image model before the current one is retired on 1 December. A named person is never drawn. live #18689
  • A daily check flags any model the code names that has a newer version, or an end date within 60 days. live #18743
  • Stored files and product analytics are pinned to the United States, with a guard so they cannot drift back. live #18617
  • A collector for YouTube, LinkedIn and Reddit runs in shadow mode, behind a switch that is off. built, off #18622
