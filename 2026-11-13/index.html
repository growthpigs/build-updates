Front 4 · found by us, fix merged not live yet

A problem we found this week

EID's energy prediction markets stopped updating in mid-October
The market fetch keeps only the first 35 of about 300 matching series. In early October a batch of new daily state gas-price series took those places, and EID's energy series fell out one by one from 12 to 14 October. Today, of 293 open energy markets for EID, only 3 big ones have a fresh price. The fix merged today: the fetch rotates through every series EID holds, stalest first, and keeps 10 places for new ones. merged, not live yet It reaches the live app with a nightly release; we check EID's prices on 14 November.
