Front 1 · the Morning Feed

The feed is audited every day, on the live feed

  • 19 November, first full audit on feed.thewarroom.ai as EID: 39 items graded. 25 passed, 11 failed, 2 behave as built, 1 could not be checked.
  • Ten changes (nine fixes and a test) merged the same day and went into the live app with that evening's release (18:44 UTC): a site that is not a news outlet no longer leads a story or takes the credit; Breaking and Rising stories show on Today's picks and no longer sit behind a tab; every early-warning label reads provisional; the Daily Brief agrees with the threat level. being checked
  • The audit has not yet recorded its re-check of those ten on the live feed, so we call them shipped, not passed.
  • Ask War Room from a feed card opens a new chat on that story, not your last conversation. A photo that fails once is tried again before its frame is removed. live since 17 Nov
  • Talk on X: an outlet's own post is no longer shown as talk about the story. on staging

Fronts 3 and 4 · supply and upkeep

More stories arrive with their text, and production checks itself

  • Refused pages: when a publisher refuses our request for a story's text, the story gets a second, paid check, up to 40 a client a day. live since 18 Nov Read on the live database today: EID used all 40 on 19 and on 20 November, so its limit rises to 150. on staging
  • Google News: a second supplier takes over when the first slows, and links the hourly run skipped are retried the next hour. on staging
  • Daily Clips: a run cut short by a deploy is rebuilt. Read on the live database today: all 6 scheduled runs since 18 November finished. live
  • Morning Report (the 6 a.m. UTC status email to Carlos and Jackson): on 17 November a deploy made it 69 minutes late. It now sends once from its own scheduled job, and arrived at 06:02, 06:03 and 06:02 UTC on 18, 19 and 20 November. live
  • Daily quality check: a read-only check that compares each client's briefs, Daily Clips and summaries with a week earlier and pages us on a drop. Its first run, on 17 November, read 8 clients: 7 were level and one was flagged for a thinner Daily Clips.
  • Public Intel names the date of its Meta snapshot instead of always saying "live". in the live app since 17 Nov
