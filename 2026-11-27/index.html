What users can do now

Try these on the live app today

  • Open the Morning Feed at feed.thewarroom.ai: breaking stories first, every card dated.
  • Send a story to a colleague as a checklist and note, from your own email.
  • Click an ad figure in the feed to see it in the public ad library.
  • Change News keywords without the feed going empty while they are checked.
  • Press Verify on a Daily Clips brief to see stories covered elsewhere that War Room does not hold.
  • Sign in to /admin with your own email and password.
