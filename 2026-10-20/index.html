Front · running cost

News search was two thirds of the AI bill

The news job asked the same keyword questions 8 times a day on the most expensive search model. In the 7 days to 16 Oct that was $302.74 of about $445 in AI spend.

  • We ran the current search and four cheaper options side by side on staging for 48 hours on staging
  • Result: the lighter model for the background search saves about $22 a day and finds about 2% fewer new articles
  • The cheapest option costs about 30 times less per call, but found about a third fewer stories, so it is not the switch we recommend yet
  • Switching production now, with a check that article counts hold this week
