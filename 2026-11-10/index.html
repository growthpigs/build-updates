Front 1 · the Morning Feed live still validating

The Morning Feed explains why a story matters

  • Evidence panels on an expanded story: who else is lobbying, the money behind it, the whip count from recorded votes, what members said then and say now, the rules paper trail, who is in the room at hearings, related federal lawsuits. A panel shows only what its sources support.
  • Tracked advertisers' live Meta ads appear on stories about the issue those ads name.
  • It follows each client's setup: US-only clients get US-subject stories; a client can watch 25 X accounts, up from 10.
  • Its own new design.
