Front 1 · what a reader sees live

Each story carries a take, a photo and its own signals

  • War Room's take, the short "what this means for you" line, is now written by Fable 5.2 and reads as War Room's view. 14 Nov edition: 5 of 5 stories had one. 15 Nov: 2 of 5; the other 3 were held back, each with a logged reason (no story text, or the take stated a fact the story does not carry). live #17360
  • Photos: every card uses the parts it has, and more cards carry a photo. 14 Nov edition: 5 of 5 EID stories had a photo, up from 2 of 5 on 13 Nov, with no duplicates. live #17645
  • Early warning: a card is flagged when local press, a surge from one organisation, or a quiet keyword picks a story up early. First seen 14 Nov at 13:38 UTC on 1 of 54 cards (the Texas Railroad Commission transition team, 3 Texas outlets). live #17977
  • The feed files what it shows: every story the feed draws goes into the client's Knowledge Base, one row per story. EID: 53 rows after the first build on 14 Nov, 54 after the second, 0 duplicates. live #17965
Front 2 · act on a story

Every card has buttons that do something

  • From a card: Copy for Claude Cowork, Talking points, a statement or brief, SWOT this story, Ask War Room, Watch, Share and Receipts (a cited one-pager of the card). In the live app since the 12 to 14 November releases. #17645
  • Our 16 November audit of every button on the live feed found three faults: a draft could take up to 27.7 s and fail, Watch refused stories added since the morning build, and 22 of the 34 cards in EID's edition had nothing under the headline. #18210
  • The three fixes reached the live app in this morning's release (05:53 UTC). On staging a draft now starts in about half a second. checked on staging not yet re-checked live PR #18215 PR #18236 PR #18245
Front 3 · found by us, fixed live since 15 Nov

No two labels on a card that cannot both be true

The sweep
A card said Early warning while the Building tab said 0. That started a sweep of every label on EID's live feed on 15 November: 14 kinds of contradiction, such as "9 h old" beside a start time 31 hours earlier, or "6 rated outlets" beside "3 of 6 rated".
The result
13 of the 14 are fixed and were checked live on EID at 19:40 UTC on 15 November. The 14th, a person's name matching a client keyword, had no story to test that day, so we do not count it yet. #18091
